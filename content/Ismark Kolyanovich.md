The older son of the deceased burgomaster of Barovia, Kolyan Indirovich, and new burgomaster after his father's death.

He has been kind and welcoming of our heroes, fighting side by side in defense of the village. He asked our heroes to escort his sister [[Ireena]] to [[Vallaki]].

Ismark wants to keep his village and Ireena safe, keep his father's memory alive, and one day match his ancestor's legacy.
