Start: Barovia graveyard

![[image - S06 - Barovia church.png|377]]
#### After the burgomaster's funeral

Everyone follows [[Grezzim]] into the church. [[Father Donavich]] is at the brink of tears, incredulous that what he thought was a monster is really still his son. 

Our group bombards [[Doru]] with questions and they learn:
- Doru bought the book *Van Richten’s Guide to Vampires* from a Vistnani caravan long ago and knows it cover to cover. He dreamed of one day destroying the Devil of Castle Ravenloft and bringing freedom to his people.
- About four and a half months ago, a foreign scholar calling himself Alanik Ray arrived in Barovia and rented a room in the burgomaster's manor. Alanik was a curious and interesting man who had a pet monkey.
- When an enormous dire wolf attacked a group of children near the forest, Doru saw "Alanik" fend it off with expert skill—and when the wolf knocked the hat off of "Alanik's" head, his appearance magically changed, revealing him to be the legendary vampire hunter Dr. [[Rudolph van Richten]].
* Doru confronted him and insisted that van Richten lead the village in revolt against Castle Ravenloft. Van Richten was reluctant at first, so much that he and Doru got into a very heated argument and van Richten ripped out the preface of Doru's copy of his book in order to make a point. *"He warned me that faith and books meant nothing in the face of true evil, I should have listened"*. After much insistence from Doru and another outsider whom they call "the wizard" van Richten agreed. 
* A mob of more than two dozen fighters, led by Doru and his best friend [[Escher]], assaulted the castle. They defeated many undead guards but when they finally opened Strahd’s crypt, Escher and other got lost in the fighting, and Strahd’s coffin was empty.
* Shortly thereafter, Strahd emerged from the shadows and attacked the mob with merciless force. Most of Doru’s companions were slain easily. Doru himself was turned into a vampire spawn, and later learned that Escher had been as well. Doru doesn’t know what happened to van Richten. 
* Doru spent nearly three months at Castle Ravenloft, during which Strahd repeatedly turtured him, forced him to drink the blood of his former companions. About two weeks ago, Strahd commanded Doru to return to the church and visit his father. 
* Doru shares information on the weaknesses and abilities of vampires.

Doru was very sad and desperate when hearing about [[Gertruda]]'s disappearance and asked the group to find her. Doru also warns the group that Strahd will likely take an active interest in newcomers to his land, and that they should therefore be careful. He tells them the location of a hidden weapons cache that van Richten kept in case of emergencies. 

Father Donavich says that maybe [[Vallaki]] is too close to Castle Ravenloft for [[Ireena]] to be safe and recommends considering the [[Abbey of St. Markovia]] in [[Krezk]] as an eventual destination. He notes it was once a bastion of good, and may yet still offer some protection.

[[Ismark Kolyanovich|Ismark]] and Ireena enter the church and carefully reunite with Doru. They tell the group to meet again at their house at noon to get ready and depart.

The group heads to [[Bildrath]]'s shop to stock up before the trip. [[Sidgey]] politely comments that the prices are insanely high, and Bildrath justifies himself by saying that times are tough for everyone. Bildrath can only accept gold and silver, he has no use for jewels but tells the group to try and sell the jewels to a travelling merchant. Sidgey buys a shield and hide armor in exchange for his old leather armor + 23 GP + 2 GP gifted by Grezzim. Maren buys a chain, a bag of caltrops and a battleaxe for 31 GP thanks to a discount negotiated by Sidgey, promising to sing praises of Bildrath's magnificent store all around the land. Legba is very sad because he can't afford what he wants and looks at Azrael with puppy eyes. Azrael tries to ignore it but in the end gives in and gives Legba 10 GP so Legba can afford the 25 GP shield. Bildrath is happy for the sales and gifts them a net which [[Maren]] keeps.

Back at the burgomaster manor, Maren and [[Legba]] go to the study with Ismark to ask him for directions and make a map with Maren's cartographer's tools, including the location of van Richten's cache told by Doru. Meanwhile, the imp misunderstood the phrase "everyone's got skeletons in their closet" and goes upstairs to try and find exciting skeletons in Ismark's closet, pulls all the contents out into the floor. [[Grezzim]] follows to clean up the [[Pishka|imp]]'s mess and notices a small diary filled with written and crossed out insults like *"~~Ismark the lesser~~"*, *"~~Ismark the loser~~"*, *"~~Ismark el pusilánime~~"* ... Ismark clearly has self esteem problems and pressure to live up to his family's expectations. Grezzim steals the diary.

![[image - S06 - Map of Barovia.png|573]]

While all that is happening, [[Azrael]] suddenly realizes the lady they're supposed to escort to Vallaki is nowhere to be found. After looking for her in the house he asks Ismark, who tells him to look for her in Lugdana's Knoll north of the village. Azrael and Sidgey go there and find Ireena kneeling under a tree apparently digging. She tells them she came to retrieve a box of heirlooms from her mother, as she doesn't know when she will be able to return. She shows them a pressed purple flower, a small wooden amulet in the shape of a bundle of flowers, and a metal bracelet encrusted with moonstone and lapislazuli. The first two mean a lot to her because they remind her of her dead mother. The bracelet was on Ireena's arm when Kolyan found her as a child near the forest, and her mother took it as a sign from the Morninglord that this was a miracle child. Ireena gives the bracelet to the group to thank them for escorting her safely. Azrael sees an inscription on the inside: "*Tatyana: May you find your path, and may you find light even in the darkest places—Mother & Father*".

They return to the house and all gather there. On the table they find 15 rations and 2 bottles of holy water, supplies from Ismark and Father Donavich for the road. The siblings say their goodbyes emotionally, and Ismark shakes everyone's hands thanking them for escorting his sister. He tells Sidgey to put the family shortsword to good use.

![[image - S06 - Bridge over River Ivlis.png|439]]

They walk outside the village, admire the views, cross the stone bridge over the river, enter a denser part of the Svalich Woods, and pay attention to the surroundings to follow Doru's instructions to van Richten’s cache. 

![[image - S06 - View towards S, Mount Ghakis.png|379]]

Maren almost gets lots but Azrael corrects the course and they find the large oak tree with a foxhole at the base. While they argue about who will get their hands inside the hole, the imp doesn't hesitate - because hesitation is defeat - and goes inside. They hear some scraping and grunting but don't see anything. Nothing. A few seconds go by and they notice there are small footsteps on the dirt, and what seems to be the tracks of an object being dragged. Azrael steps on the object to stop it, and both the imp and a small wooden chest become visible. Azrael brags to Grezzim that he has two sets of thieves' tools to open it vs. Grezzim's zero, and proceeds to fail to open a simple lock. Angered, Azrael throws the chest to the ground, breaking it. Amongst the remains of the wooden box they find 10 silvered arrows, a light crossbow, 1 healer's kit, 1 vial of holy water (and another one broken), 1 potions of healing (and another one broken) and 5 strange arrows with some contraption at the head. 

![[image - S06 - Oak tree with foxhole.png|439]]

They take the chance to use Sidgey's disguise kit and disguise Ireena as a dark skinned woman with very curly hair, in case anyone is hunting for her. The trick pays off, as they soon meet Kereza who doesn't recognize Ireena and believes it when she's introduced as Sidgey's cousin. Kereza tells them the search party sent after Gertruda wasn't successful and almost half of them died. She's currently looking for the Lansten family (a couple, their 11 y/o daughter and their 6 y/o twins). They haven't been seen since the night of the attack, but with the news of the other search party losing more people, Kereza needs to stop the search and head back. 

They continue along the road with Legba and Maren leading the column. Soon Legba and Maren spot 5 wandering zombies in the distance, two large and three small zombies. Clearly the Lansten family. Maren suggests just letting them wander off but all the others seem to be super eager to fight. They ambush the zombies and very easily dispatch them. Grezzim makes a small tomb marker and leaves it along with what little clothes remained from the Lanstens, to inform whoever comes searching next that they can stop the search.

![[image - S06 - Dense misty Svalich woods.png|426]]

As they progress on the road they see the intersection called the River Ivlis crossroads, where a second road splits off to the right. They take the road to the right and soon after hear horseshoes in the distance, along with the wheels of a carriage. They all jump to hide by the side of the road, and Legba pulls Ireena who seemed to be frozen with fear. The carriage approaches, stops in the road right in front of them, and the driver walks around to open the carriage door. [[Strahd von Zarovich|An elegantly dressed man]] walks out of the carriage, the driver bows to him, the elegant man walks towards where our group is hiding and says "Lady Kolyana".
