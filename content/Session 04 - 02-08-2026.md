Start: Dining Hall in the Durst Manor basement

Our heroes discuss what has happened during the time they have been in this house, memories are blurry. They debate whether to rest or not, and decide to push forward, in the direction that smells horribly rotten.

They are immediately attacked by a pack of ghouls, but using Maren as a human meatshield, they manage to keep the ghouls at bay and defeat them. The imp made a heroic rush to feed Maren a healing potion when Maren most needed it, and Grezzim felt very proud. Then they decide that maybe it is after all a good idea to take a short rest.

While resting they continue exchanging views on what has happened, and Azrael generously gives half of his silver arrows to Maren. Azrael closely inspects three scrolls he found inside the chest that contained the letter, and can determine they are spell scrolls of a type of magic he cannot wield, but Legba can. Legba now has the scrolls.

The clock ticks 22:00.

#### The Shrine
They continue along the corridor and find a large room, where old moldy skeletons are chained to the walls with rusty iron, there are shadows burned into the walls, and where there is a large wooden statue of a man wearing a dark cape. The statue of the man is petting the statue of a wolf with one hand and holding a smokey-looking orb on the other. 

Sidgey is very curious about the orb and approaches to touch it. As he does, he hears voices telling him to stop and leave. Probably nothing, so he grabs the orb. The shadows burned into the walls hop to life and start moving towards Sidgey, ignoring everyone else. Combat starts, and the shadows try to gang up on Sidgey, who thinks quickly and conjures a minor illusion, tricking the shadows into thinking he threw the orb away. This clever trick may have saved Sidgey's life, but he still suffered grievous wounds that reduced his STR by -2. Our greedy heroes defeat the shadows and push onwards, not before breaking one arm of the statue, blindfolding it and covering the orb with a coat.

They find a secret door exiting this room, Maren pushes it open and finds that it is a secret passage back to the wolf den on the 1st floor.

They split up and carry on: Sidgey, Azrael and Grezzim move towards a fresh humid breeze, whereas Legba and Maren investigate a nearby room.

#### Sleeping quarters
They find 5 small rooms with straw mattresses and a small chest in each. 

Grezzim and the imp open a chest, where the imp found a black leather eyepatch embedded with a red carnelian and wears it proudly, laughing like a crazy pirate.

Sidgey is fast and manages to loot three unlocked chests, finding there is small loot inside a pouch made of human skin; 3 pieces of agate neatly folded in a black cloth as well as an ivory hairbrush with silver bristles.

Azrael is struggling with the fanciest looking chest which has a proper strong lock, but manages with his thieves' tools. When he opens it, he finds a well kept notebook containing Drasha's notes. It has the names of people, many different types of torture they suffered, and whether or not they were "fed to Walter". When reading this, they now know for sure that Walter is the beast and that the human sacrifices feed him.

#### Cult leader's room
Maren and Legba enter first what seems to be a small sitting room, with a table and two chairs, then the bedroom next to it. In the bedroom there is a wardrobe with many cloaks, a solid bed, and a chest. Inside the chest Maren finds many things, which Maren passes one by one to Legba:
- a cloak made of exotic fabric which is light but tough, with a magical aura but with patches made of human skin that make it look creepy, disgusting and unsettling. It is in reality an adventurer's cloak of protection, the cult trapped and killed the adventurer then perverted the cloak with human skin patches. It does give +1 to AC and saving throws like the original, but because it is perverted and disgusting it applies a -1 penalty to all CHA rolls (checks, saving throws, attack rolls, spell save DC).
- 4 potions of healing
- 1 bottle of alchemist's fire
- 1 bullseye lantern that smells very bad (it is fueled with human grease and also applies a -1 penalty to all CHA for whoever is near it when it is outside and stinking)
- 1 pandero, the frame seems to have been of good construction, but the membrane is human skin and instead of metal zills it has bones. It also applies a -1 penalty to all CHA
- 1 thieves' tools
- 1 book, with a yellow leather cover. It is a spellbook from some wizard the cult trapped containing: lv1: disguise self, identify, mage armor, magic missile, protection from evil and good; lv2: darkvision, hold person, invisibility, magic weapon

Once Maren has emptied the chest, something squishy remains at the bottom. [[Image - Boneless.png|Something]] very creepy jumps at them from the bottom of the chest. Maren and Legba shout as loud as they can and combat starts.

The monster seems to be the animated boneless skin of Gustav. Sidgey, Azrael and Grezzim hear the shouting and rush to help. Maren barricades the doorway with the table and Legba sets fire to it. They defeat the boneless without much trouble and have a look at the loot. Sidgey tells Legba to throw away the creepy pandero because Sidgey is not crazy. They carry on, following the rhytmic rumbling into a staircase leading further down. Azrael continues to be disturbed by the rhytmic rumbling.

#### The dungeon level
The clock ticks 23:00.

They reach what seems to be a large room with many columns, but also with alcoves carved in the stone. In each one of them there is some creepy talisman or offering, among them:
- a small mummified goblin hand
- a rusty dagger with a rat's skull in the pommel
- a polished orb made of a nothic's eye
- a mushroom carved from bone
- a dessicated frog at the end of a stick like a magic wand
- a bag of bat guano

Like with the perverted cloak of protection and the pandero, it is clear the cult thought they were being badass and cool with their artifacts but they are just creepy lame losers.

There are two exits to this room: a slope towards murky water, blocked by a heavy portcullis, and a corridor. They follow the corridor to what seems to be a dungeon with many cells, in which there are rusty shackles and skeletons. They find a gold ring on one of the skeletons, and they also find a secret door towards the room with water, which they enter.

#### The altar
The room is large, with stone pillars supporting the roof, and with murky water covering most of the floor. Stairs lead up to dry stone ledges that hug the walls. In the middle of the room, more stairs rise to form an octagonal dais that also rises above the water. Rusty chains with shackles dangle from the ceiling directly above a stone altar mounted on the dais. The altar is carved with hideous depictions of grasping ghouls and is stained with dry blood. A small, white bundle lies atop it, surrounded by pulsating, fleshy tendrils. The tendrils run to a breach in the far wall that leads to a dark cave, their fleshy masses connecting to a dark, hulking shadow that lies within, its bloated mass rising and falling with a slow, shuddering rhythm.

Maren threw Sidgey to the altar and threw him so hard that he almost overshoots and lands in the water on the other side. Once there, the rumbling stops and Sidgey sees that the words "FEED HIM" are carved into the flat stone surface of the altar just below the white bundle, surrounded by several fleshy tendrils inset with human teeth. The bundle is the size and shape of a baby wrapped in a cloth. Sidgey unwraps the bundle and finds only a rusted, serrated dagger stained with ancient blood. 

When the group debates whether to offer a sacrifice to the altar, Grezzim quickly dismisses the imp to a pocket dimension to avoid them getting any crazy ideas. Impatient at the lack of decision, Sidgey stabs the tendrils with both his daggers. The [[Flesh mound|dark shadow]] in the corner of the room moves and wakes up.

It is an intense battle, with the flesh mound at some point eating Sidgey, Sidgey being rescued, then the flesh mound eating Azrael, and both Maren and Legba downed at some point. When the party defeats the flesh mound, it shrieks loudly to reveal a ribcage and a baby inside, and fights again. After a very tense encounter, Grezzim delivers the final blow.

#### The escape
The ghost of Elisabeth appears and tells the heroes that even if they defeated her pet they won't escape the house, and the house begins to crumble. They rush trying to escape. 

At some point, Gustav's ghost blocks their way and asks them to remain and die as an offering to appease Elisabeth, but they resist and Legba persuades Gustav to move away by telling him they are helping his children.

They continue their escape and are surrounded by a very large number of shadows who continue their cultist chants. The team continues rushing out even while the shadows are constantly hitting and damaging them, there is no time to lose. 

The team manages to exit the house just moments before it crumbles to the ground. They almost can't believe how lucky they were. When the dust settles and they breathe a little, they realize they are in an unfamiliar place, in the middle of the woods with a single road in sight. They follow the road until the find a dilapidated stone tower. 

They finally put the Durst children's bones to rest, although, strangely, Legba cannot see that their spirits move on anywhere. It is finally time to rest and level up.
