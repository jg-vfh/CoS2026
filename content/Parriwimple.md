Parpol Cantemir, called Parriwimple by everyone, is a very tall and strong young man. After his parents' death, he lives with his uncle Bildrath and works at his shop. He is a kind and helpful man.

Parriwimple is nervous and fearful, but not a coward. He overcame his fear to help in the defenses during the siege of Barovia.
