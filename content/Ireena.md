Ireena Kolyana is the younger daughter of the deceased burgomaster Kolyan Indirovich. She was adopted as a young girl but both Kolyan and Ismark have always treated her like their blood. 

During the siege of Barovia, the night her father was killed, Ireena seems to have been bitten in the neck by Strahd. 

She will be escorted to Vallaki by our heroes to seek refuge there.
