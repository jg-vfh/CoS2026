Start: Dilapidated stone tower on the forest road

#### Dilapidated tower
Grezzim wakes to grunting noises to find Sidgey practicing battle moves with Maren's shield. Sidgey says he's done singing the epic tales of better men, it's time for the cover band to write original hits. 

#### Svalich woods
They wander through the misty woods and Legba whiffs the stench of death. They find a dead body carrying a compass, seems to have died of exhaustion. A nearby tree has 13 scratch marks that appear to be a counter, and an arrow - they follow. They find a dead horse next to another tree with 13 marks, the compass needle is going crazy. Azrael decides to take the compass and go into the forest, as he walks deeper into the forest he is engulfed by the mist and almost chokes, gets 1 level of exhaustion. They continue following the arrows carved on the trees, eventually they have to cross thick mist and when they do ... they find the same body again.

They decide to return to the road they were originally on and continue towards the valley. They cross a large gate which states "Barovia". They cross and soon see a village in the distance. The villagers are fortifying some very rough barricades, they seem hostile and ask whether our group is dead or living. Our group is not very eloquent when answering, and Maren is hungry. A man who seems to be in charge arrives and defuses the tension, inviting our group inside the improvised city walls.

#### Village of Barovia
The man introduces himself as [[Ismark Kolyanovich]], son of the burgomaster of the village. He leads them through a street crowded with injured and seemingly homeless people, signs of violence and destruction. They pass the town square, where there is a statue to a hero named ISMARK ANTONOVICH THE GREAT, Legba asks and it is an ancestor of this Ismark, a vampire hunter hero who died rescuing travelers from a direwolf. They notice a raven with blue feathers on one wing looking at them from the rooftops.

![[image - Ismark.png|489]]

They enter the Blood of the Vine tavern. Ismark offers them all food and drink. He says that the mists that surround the land cannot be escaped. He explains that the village is under siege by undead monsters since a local villager called Doru, the son of the village priest, led an uprising against Castle Ravenloft about three months ago, and that every night for the last two weeks they have been attacked. 

A man on the next table curses Ismark and tells the group that they should not place their trust in Ismark the Lesser. Legba sits with the angry man and learns his name is [[Bildrath]] Cantemir, he is the owner of the town shop and tells a bit more of the story. After Doru organized a raid to attack "the devil" in Castle Ravenloft and failed, a dark skinned elf came to the village to proclaim that they would be punished. People were rushing to flee towards Vallaki but Ismark gave an "inspiring" speech and convinced many to stay and defend their village. Bildrath wanted to leave, but his sister Marta and her husband Dragomir decided to stay so he stayed too. Marta and Dragomir died in the undead attacks, and now Bildrath is responsible for his nephew Parriwimple. Bildrath is angry at Ismark and hopeless about the town's future.

![[image - Bildrath.png|492]]

A woman enters, screaming desperately. Her name is Mary and her daughter Gertruda ran away, probably towards Vallaki to request help. Her daughter was Doru's fiancee. Ismark and Bildrath argue loudly almost getting into a fight. The players offer to look for her but Ismark says he will send more experienced hunters and rangers, but could use help manning the defenses at night to cover for the people that will go search for Gertruda. Asks them to get liquid fire from his sister back at his house and pays the bill.

They go to the burgomaster's mansion to retrieve liquid fire. An angry mob is there yelling at an old and injured man, the burgomaster. From what the characters overhear, the angry mob believes a superstition that the devil likes redheads and wants to give him the burgomaster's daughter [[Ireena]] as a gift, they also do not consider Ireena a Barovian because she is adopted. The burgomaster argues, Legba interferes and they manage to defuse the situation.

![[image - Ireena.png|455]]

Inside the mansion they speak with the burgomaster Kolyan Indirovich, about their past and the town's situation. Grezzim is a bit mistrusting but comes to realize that Kolyan is genuinely welcoming and a kind man. The burgomaster has kind words for everyone and gives Azrael a letter of introduction with the family seal, so that the burgomaster's friends in other barovian towns will offer them hospitality. They get crates of bottles of liquid fire from Ireena and take them back to Ismark, Ireena will stay home with her injured father.

Back at the town square, Ismark takes one crate for himself and the west gate, gives a crate to the angry archer from the barricade earlier on, Kereza, who will lead the defense of the north side of town, and sends our characters with the last crate of liquid fire to reinforce a warrior named Korga on the east barricade. When they arrive they see a man who seems to be injured giving instructions to a big man almost as tall as Maren, who is trying and failing to hammer massive stakes onto the ground Maren intervenes and easily finishes staking the poles to reinforce the gate. Impressed, Korga gives his barricade lead armband to Maren and asks the characters what their talents are and how they can contribute to the defense. They notice that Bildrath is here helping, and the large man who couldn't hammer seems to have a teenage face and is his nephew [[Parriwimple]].

![[image - Parriwimple.png|321]]

#### Defense of the East barricade
They all take what they find useful from the large basket of gear Parriwimple gathered earlier today. Legba takes a light crossbow, Maren keeps the warhammer from earlier, takes a battleaxe and gives her quarterstaff to Grezzim, and all but Maren take a spear. Azrael positions himself on a rooftop close to Grezzim, Legba goes to another rooftop next to archer Miruska, while Maren and Sidgey stand atop the barricade with Bildrath and Parriwimple. The injured Kerga is below, blocking a possible breach and offering to hand them tools or fire bottles.

When night falls the undead start coming wave after wave. Parriwimple is scared but still surprises everyone with his incredible strength wielding a pike, whereas Bildrath seems calm and brave. Eager to get killed, Maren and Sidgey jump to the ground outside the barricade to fight the undead head-on. The others keep the zombies at bay with their ranged attacks. At some point a few ghouls join the zombies and the battle grows intense. The imp goes invisible, throws a bottle of alchemist's fire at the zombies and then pushes Azrael off the roof. Legba makes a few of the zombies flee the battle. The zombies manage to destroy parts of the barricade but the defenders kill them before they can do much else. Then someone shoots arrows from the mists and Miruska is badly injured. 

![[image - Pishka pushes Azrael.jpg|584]]

The last attack wave is a creepy looking zombie that drools red vapor, and a badass looking wight who seems intelligent and wields a longbow and a longsword. Things get more difficult, Sidgey is knocked unconscious and Parriwimple is scared. Legba heals Sidgey, Grezzim and Azrael continue being effective snipers with powerful spells, Bildrath motivates Parriwimple, and Maren hammers zombies around. The red vapor zombie comes closer and all nearby feel overwhelmed by his putrid smell. It vomits some red mist that slightly damages many defenders and kills Korga. A short while later, Korga rises as a zombie. Meanwhile, the wight walks up to Azrael and touches him, sapping his max HP before calmly retreating. The defenders are angry and will not let anyone escape, they eliminate all undead, with a bit of help from Parriwimple who accidentally dropped holy water on the zombie Korga. Grezzim loots a studded leather armor from the wight, and Azrael loots a longbow that he gives to Maren.

When they think the're in the clear, the raven with one blue wing comes desperate and pulls Sidgey's scarf. Maren tries calming it but the raven insists, Grezzim speaks to it and hears that there is an emergency at the town square. They all run as fast as they can. When they arrive to the town square they can see all the refugees behind the last set of makeshift barriers, and a few meters further west, Ismark is the lone survivor of the west gate defenders. Sidgey is the first to reach Ismark, singing an inspiring song, and Ismark throws his shortsword at Sidgey to form a defensive line together. In front of them, another red vapor zombie walking straight towards the villagers in the square, and a wight sniping with his longbow from afar.

#### Defense of the town square
Everyone joins the fight. Maren rushes to get beside Ismark and protect him with her shield, Ismark and Sidgey attack the zombie with swords while the others attack from range. The red zombie doesn't seem interested in fighting here, it continues moving straight to where all the villagers are. Grezzim does something strange, grows confident and powerful, looks very scary, the imp notices and seems to behave more seriously. Everyone focuses attacks on the red zombie and Maren tries to snipe the wight, joined by Kereza who finished defending the north and came to help. With everyone's efforts, they kill the red mist zombie and the wight flees.

When they think they're safe, a dark-skinned elf riding a large horse comes into town, escorted by 12 zombies. Grezzim rushes to kneel before him and the rider looks at him with disdain. The rider goes all the way towards Ismark, takes out a scroll and reads: 

	*"Four score and seven days past, this people rose in unlawful rebellion against Strahd von Zarovich, Count of Barovia, Master of Castle Ravenloft, and Protector of the Balinok Mountains. For this treasonous act, this settlement has been met with righteous punishment, so that its inhabitants might learn the weight of their defiance.*
	
	*"Witnessed by the eyes of the dead and damned, this price has been extracted in blood, common and noble alike. Let it be known that on this day, the mercy of Strahd von Zarovich extends once more unto those who return to their fief and field—while those who continue in their folly shall be swept away as chaff before the wind."*

![[image - Rahadin.png|281]]

[[Rahadin]] dismounts, walks to Ismark and says *“Please accept my congratulations on your new position—and my condolences on your loss."* As Rahadin leaves, Legba and Ismark realize Ismark's father must be dead. Everyone rushes to Ismark's house.

Ismark arrives first and when our group arrives they hear a bloodcurling scream: Ismark found his dad dead in a pool of blood, and Ireena on the ground nearby. Our group checks the bodies: Kolyan died by a massive slash to his chest, whereas Ireena is still alive but has two dot-sized wounds on her neck. She regains consciousness and tells that she left the house to deliver more fire bottles and when she came home she heard Kolyan welcoming someone into the house, in a monotone voice. Azrael recognizes this as the clear signs of a person under a charm spell. Ismark and Ireena need to go and help the injured villagers, our group offers to clean up and prepare Kolyan's body.

The group distrusts Grezzim after seeing him kneel for Rahadin and getting confirmation that his lord is el mismísimo Strahd. Sidgey helps Legba and Maren clean up while Azrael and Grezzim go upstairs to rest, each in a different room. Grezzim sneaks around and finds no signs of suspicious activity. Azrael finds pages torn out of a book in his room with very interesting obscure information. Sidgey goes up to his room. Legba and Maren hide when they hear Ismark return and overhear a conversation between Ismark and Ireena. Seems as if Ismark wants to send Ireena away to a nearby town, Vallaki, fearing for her safety. Ireena is angry and doesn't want to be treated like a child, but agrees on the condition that they give their father a proper burial.

#### The aftermath of the attack
Next morning, Ismark asks for three favors: escorting Ireena to Vallaki, seeking a Vistana seer called Madam Eva to ask for advice in fighting the devil, and help with carrying their father's coffin to the graveyard for a proper burial. The group agrees and they start by carrying the coffin to the graveyard. On the way they meet Parriwimple, carrying supplies back and forth, who mentions a man with a monkey who was Doru's friend, and invites them to come to the shop before leaving town.

Once at the graveyard they enter the church to ask the priest Father Donavich to do the burial rites. The priest says he hasn't been able to communicate with the Morninglord since Doru took his sacred symbol in his failed rebellion. Legba convinces Donavich that the two of them together can do the rites with Legba's sacred symbol. Meanwhile, the invisible imp sees a suspicious door in the floor behind the altar and Grezzim sneaks to stay behind and investigate when the others leave. 

![[image - Father Donavich.png|285]]

Grezzim tries to open the lock but fails, and in his desperation and with no real help from the imp, breaks his thieves' tools. He decides this is too important for stealth and breaks the lock. Inside the basement he hears footsteps on the walls and ceiling, and when he goes in he sees a thin and ill looking young man in the corner of the room, with a sun-shaped medallion around his neck. It is [[Doru]], who seems to have been turned into a vampire and asks Grezzim to kill him to end his suffering. They talk, Grezzim encourages Doru and tries to convince him to continue living and serve his lord, Doru wants Grezzim to help him test his willpower and kill him if he fails. Doru asks Grezzim to cut his own hand and offer him blood. When Grezzim does it, Doru struggles but manages to resist his urges and his fangs recede. Doru cries with relief and is grateful to Grezzim, gives him the talisman and asks that he gives it to Donavich.

![[image - Doru.png]]

Outside, the group lowers the coffin into the hole dug by Maren and Donavich leads the prayers. Legba and Donavich cast the rites to consecrate the burial place, and the old burgomaster is put to peaceful rest. Kolyan's children are thankful and sentimental. Grezzim comes running with the talisman in hand, tells what happened, and all rush back to the church.
