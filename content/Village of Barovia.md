The village of Barovia is the easternmost settlement in the valley of Barovia, overlooked by Castle Ravenloft.

The current burgomaster is [[Ismark Kolyanovich]], after the death of his father Kolyan Indirovich.

People we know here are:
- [[Ismark Kolyanovich]]
- [[Bildrath]] Cantemir
- [[Parriwimple]]
- [[Father Donavich]]
- [[Doru]]

Places and shops here are:
- Blood of the vine tavern
- Bildrath's mercantile
- Barovia Church
