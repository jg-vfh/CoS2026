Start: Marlbor, near Daggerford

# Intro - at Marlbor
PCs meet in tavern. We have [[Grezzim]], [[Maren]], [[Papa Legba|Legba]], [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] and [[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]]. Person called Marten Boyd recruits them on behalf of Mayor Hastor Nemzowitch. They go and wait for like over 1 hr in the lobby. [[Grezzim]] talks to the guards and one of them, Ranulf, tells the party that his cousin Gundalf is one of the missing guards and hurries the mayor. 

They talk to the mayor, hear about the problems in Willow Creek including stolen and missing animals, blighted crops and a kidnapped bastard baby. A 4-guard patrol was sent but never returned. They are offered 60, no, sorry, 50 GP for investigating the issues, [[Azrael]] tries to negotiate arrogantly and gets it down to 45 GP. 

# Travel to Willow Creek
Walk towards Willow Creek, find blockade on the road and the corpses of the 4 guards. Combat against various blights. [[Maren]] finds a shield and [[Grezzim]] finds what appears to be a love letter from “Y” addressed to Gundalf. [[Grezzim]] opens the letter and feels compelled to go back to Marlbor and report that there are no problems, and he almost convinces the party. They take a long rest to heal and talk around the fire to get to know each other, and in the morning [[Grezzim]] no longer feels so strongly about going back to Marlbor.

# Gossip at Willow Creek
Arriving at Willow Creek they are ==not interested in the three children playing on the street==, then enter Annie’s Tavern. [[Annie the Barmaid]] greets them, she’s the sole owner and only employee, polite but busy. She tells them about animals being stolen and massacred, including pets. Tells them of blight / plague hitting the Blankovitch Farm.

[[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]] approaches a table with 3 men gambling and with [[Grezzim]]’s help he breaks the ice and joins the game. He hears from [[Toothless Tom]] that the [[Astrid Blankovitch]], owner of the Blankovitch Farm and otherwise the most beautiful woman in the village, is looking very sick and in very bad condition. [[Toothless Tom]] also tells them that in his job as nightwatchman he saw strange lights in the graveyard at night.

[[Maren]] approaches a table with 3 women gossiping and thanks to [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]]’s insensitive comment and slapping him, is invited to join the group. She hears that farm and pet animals have been disappearing. She also hears that the owner of the Dairy Do, Norbert Karovar, was angry and yelling at his employee Igor Tratnov worse than usual. Asking if anyone new has come by the village, an old lady tells them no one new, only [[Old Olga|Olga the Herbalist]] who has always been around.

The party continues along the road and visits Dairy Do. They see Igor Tratnov, a very ugly man with a rodent like nose, half a missing ear and very yellow rotten teeth. [[Maren]] goes to investigate the goat pen, very few goats. They meet the owner Norbert Karovar who is standoffish and arrogant. While getting a tour of the premises, [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] and [[Grezzim]] both find the crime scene of a murdered goat in the workshop. [[Papa Legba|Legba]] investigates and sees it’s a ritualistic sacrifice where they took the heart. [[Grezzim]] takes Igor apart and they have a duel of servilism, they bond and Igor tells him he was here last night, saw a hooded figure do the murder but didn’t want to say anything fearing more abuse for not intervening. [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] checks that the lock wasn’t broken, but can’t see if it was magically opened.

They start walking towards the Blankovitch Farm. Rows of apple trees on both sides of the road, and many trees are rotten or dead. They, as expected, struggle with a door. [[Maren]] goes around the house and finds two starving sows, she feeds them her rations and gains their trust. [[Grezzim]] fails but [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] succeeds in opening the lock, the door is boarded and they blast it. They investigate the house and find the couple laying in bed in almost catatonic state. Both the Blankovitches transform into vargouilles and a fight erupts. During the fight, [[Grezzim]] falls to 0 HP, is healed by [[Maren]], then falls again and is healed by [[Sidgey Songgiver]]. Two scarecrows come running into the room and attack the vargouilles. The party is victorious after all the chaos and investigate clues.

They find Astrid’s diary stating she had bought a beauty salve from [[Old Olga|Olga the Herbalist]] but it gave her pimples. [[Papa Legba|Legba]] found the vial and investigated it, convinced it was not medicine. [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] can conclude the vial had contained malicious magic.

When they’re investigating all this, a raven enters the door and yells, with the voice of a woman, “Take that [[Old Olga|Olga]]!”

Session ends.
