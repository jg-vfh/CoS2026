Start: Blankovitch Farm

# Continued at Blankovitch Farm
The adventurers recapped last session and drafted a plan. [[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]] was so shocked about the horrible events that happened to the Blankovitches that he was struggling to speak.

They decide to check out the cemetery today and then go and rest as they are all tired and damaged.

# Cemetery
They pass by the cemetery and decide to investigate reports by [[Toothless Tom]] regarding strange lights. Before getting to the cemetery, [[Maren]] asks [[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]] to take the rescued pigs from Blankovitch Farm to a safe place, and [[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]] decides to take them to Annie's Inn.

While investigating the graveyard, they split into two groups. [[Maren]] and [[Grezzim]] investigate the far end of the cemetery where there seems to be a crypt, while [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] and [[Papa Legba|Legba]] hide among the trees to observe who comes.

[[Maren]] helps [[Grezzim]] climb the roof of the crypt and he sees through the cracks in the ceiling, four strange figures dancing in a circle. 

[[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] and [[Papa Legba|Legba]] hear footsteps and hide. They see [[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]] approaching with the two pigs. [[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]] says Annie's was closed and no [[Annie the Barmaid|Annie]] was nowhere to be found.

Once entering the crypt, they see a human figure wearing a red hood next to a stone altar. They found [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] mid ritual with [[Annie the Barmaid|Annie]] on the sacrificial altar, about to kill her. They try and fail to talk their way into the situation and combat starts. Annie breaks the circle of salt and the four dretches attack the party.

After a long and tense battle where the party dispatched most of the dretches, [[Maren]] rescued [[Annie the Barmaid|Annie]] from the stone altar and [[Grezzim]] charmed [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] and persuaded her out of her aggro state using his charms and his forgery kit. [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] told the party her situation, how [[Old Olga|Olga]] kidnapped her baby and threatened to kill it unless [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] made her a potion of youth and beauty. [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] has been gathering the ingredients and a human heart was the last one she needs.

The party convince [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] to spare [[Annie the Barmaid|Annie]] and try to trick [[Old Olga|Olga]] with their help instead. They  will try to give her the unfinished potion and bolt out. They split for the night, with [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] accompanying [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] to her place and the others taking [[Annie the Barmaid|Annie]] to hers.

# Annie's Inn
They bring [[Annie the Barmaid|Annie]] back to her place, tell her they found her unconscious by the river, and she offers free lodging. Everyone rests and nothing unusual happens during the night.

# Yvonne's House
[[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] tries to hit on [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] but she sends very clear signals that she's not interested. [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] then snoops around the house, seeing a lot of herbs and potions, and resists the temptation of stealing anything. Good boy. They rest and nothing unusual happens during the night.

# Sirens in the woods
They meet up outside [[Annie the Barmaid|Annie]]'s and hatch up a plan: [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] and [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] will go far at the front, pretending he's her guide of the woods, and will give [[Old Olga|Olga]] the incomplete potion. The others will follow far behind to help in case things to bad. 

While making their way towards [[Old Olga|Olga]]'s, they narrowly avoid an encounter with creepy sirens, and [[Papa Legba|Legba]] pulls a hypnotized [[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]] back before he can get into trouble.

# Olga's Hut
[[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] and [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] reach a clearing with weird plants and a hut, the others stay in the woods. While [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] and [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] talk to [[Old Olga|Olga]], [[Papa Legba|Legba]] hears something in the treetops and spots an imp who's been spying on them. 

After some resistance from [[Old Olga|Olga]], [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] agrees to enter the house alone with her to make the trade. Meanwhile, [[Maren]] and [[Papa Legba|Legba]] notice that the imp seems to talk to a tree, and deduce that the imp communicates with [[Old Olga|Olga]].

[[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] exits [[Old Olga|Olga]]'s hut in a rush with her baby in arms and runs away. The party takes very long before entering the hut and discovering [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] unconscious on the ground: the Yvonne they saw running away was really [[Old Olga|Olga]] in disguise. [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] chases [[Old Olga|Olga]], [[Maren]] chases the imp, [[Papa Legba|Legba]] fights the blights, and [[Grezzim]] loots the hut. [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] regains consciousness and joins the chase.

After a very long and tense chase, [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]] keeps on sorcerous blasting the hag with no concerns for the baby she's carrying, the others defeat the blights and [[Maren]] subdues the imp. The trees in the clearing start to move and look aggressive. The imp says he's [[Old Olga|Olga]]'s familiar, not super happy with his treatment, and helps the party find out that [[Old Olga|Olga]] is going towards "the rat place", and flying high guides them in the right direction. [[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]] casts a spell that allows them to travel very fast.

# Wererat's den
Everyone comes to a forest clearing where there are some goats in an improvised goat pen. It seems like [[Igor Tratnov|Igor]] lives here, he's a wererat and he's been stealing goats from the dairy farm.

[[Igor Tratnov|Igor]] comes out of the den with two giant rats and two rat swarms. The party convince him to join forces against [[Old Olga|Olga]], he is persuaded by their words and because the imp confirms [[Old Olga|Olga]] has been selling [[Igor Tratnov|Igor]] fake potions against his curse. [[Igor Tratnov|Igor]] tells them the layout of the den, as well as the location of a secret entrance. They plan to attack [[Old Olga|Olga]] from two fronts and rescue the baby before she eats it. Final confrontation with [[Old Olga|Olga]]. Everyone gangs up on her mercilessly. [[Maren]] rescues the baby, [[Grezzim]] deals the killing blow. 

[[Igor Tratnov|Igor]] and [[Grezzim]] have a heart-to-heart conversation where [[Grezzim]] offers him and his rats a new home with his master, but [[Igor Tratnov|Igor]] instead feels responsible for giving his rat pack a better life in here. [[Grezzim]] seems inspired by [[Igor Tratnov|Igor]]'s conviction. Everyone agrees to keeping [[Igor Tratnov|Igor]]'s and [[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]]'s secrets so they can continue living their lives in the village.

[[Young Yvonne|Yvonne]] gives them two rewards: a [[Look Harder!|potion]] and a [[Look Harder!|spell scroll]] inside a nice scroll case, says the scroll was a gift from the baby's father who said it would help her protect the baby in case the worst would happen. They all go back to [[Annie the Barmaid|Annie]]'s tavern, where they eat and rest.

# Heading back
While on the way, a very thick fog falls upon them and they have no visibility. They think they must be halfway back to Marlbor. It starts getting chilly, the wind picks up, they can barely see past their own noses. 

Suddenly, [[Sidgey Songgiver|Sidgey]]'s scarf flies away almost as if it had a will of its own, and [[Azrael Noctis|Azrael]]'s pendant starts tugging him in the same direction and humming. They follow, come upon a house and decide to enter to seek shelter. They see a family portrait in the wall, suddenly all doors close by themselves and creepy text appears on the wall.

Session ends.
